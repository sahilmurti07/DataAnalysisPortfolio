# 🚀 RFM Customer Segmentation Analysis | BigQuery + SQL

**End-to-end RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis** that automatically segments 287 customers into 10 high-impact business groups — from **"Can't Lose Them"** to **"Lost"**.

Transformed raw 2025 transaction data into actionable insights for marketing, sales, and retention teams.

## 🎯 Business Problem Solved
Most businesses waste budget on "spray-and-pray" marketing.  
This project answers the **real questions**:
- Who are our highest-value customers?
- Who is about to churn?
- Where should we focus retention campaigns?

**Result**: Clear, automated segments that marketing & sales teams can use **immediately**.

## 📊 Key Results
| Metric                  | Value      |
|-------------------------|------------|
| Total Customers         | **287**    |
| Total Orders            | **994**    |
| Total Sales             | **₹17.07K** |
| Average Order Value     | **₹3.46**  |

**Top Insight**:  
Just **24.4%** of customers (70 people) in the top 3 segments ("Can't Lose Them" + "Champions" + "Loyal VIP") drive the majority of revenue.

**Full segment distribution** available in the dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Database**: Google BigQuery
- **Language**: Pure SQL (scalable & production-ready)
- **Scoring**: NTILE(10) + Custom business logic
- **Visualization**: Google Data Studio / Looker Studio (included)
- **Automation**: Ready for monthly refresh via scheduled queries
